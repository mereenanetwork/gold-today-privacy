# Privacy Policy for Gold Today

**Last updated:** December 29, 2024

## Introduction

Gold Today ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.**

Gold Today is designed to be privacy-friendly:
- No user accounts or registration
- No personal data collection
- No tracking or analytics
- No advertisements
- No location tracking
- No access to device storage or files

## Data We Process

The app performs the following operations:

### API Requests
- The app fetches current gold prices from MetalpriceAPI.com
- These requests do not include any personal information
- Your IP address may be visible to the API provider as part of standard internet communication
- We do not log, store, or track these requests

### Local Processing
- Currency conversion calculations are performed on your device
- Weight unit conversions are performed on your device
- No data is sent to our servers (we don't have servers)

## Third-Party Services

### MetalpriceAPI
Gold Today uses MetalpriceAPI.com to fetch real-time gold prices. When you use the app:
- A request is sent to MetalpriceAPI to get current prices
- Your IP address may be visible to MetalpriceAPI as part of normal internet traffic
- We recommend reviewing MetalpriceAPI's privacy policy at: https://metalpriceapi.com/privacy

### No Other Third Parties
We do not use:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Social media integrations
- Crash reporting tools
- Any other third-party services

## Data Storage

The app does not store any data on our servers because we don't operate any servers. All processing happens locally on your device.

## Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is suitable for all ages.

## Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal data, there is no personal data to:
- Access
- Delete
- Modify
- Export

## Internet Permission

The app requires internet permission solely to:
- Fetch current gold prices from MetalpriceAPI
- No other network activity occurs

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.
